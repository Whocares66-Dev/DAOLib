A note from `C:\project\DAOParagonEngine` (paragon-agent, 2026-10-17), for DAOLib's owner to keep, move or drop. Paragon is DA:O on Bevy (64-bit); it links `daolib_core` x64 through a C ABI shim of its own (`native/daolib_shim.cpp` there) and reads nothing of the formats itself. Milestone 1 is done over core as it stands: `install::Index`, `model::MeshOf`/`HierarchyOf`, `level::RoomOf`, `mao::Read` draw the Circle tower (`lak303d`, 9 rooms, 1,201 placed models, 1,404 meshes, textures and lightmaps) in 0.2 s. ## What the files hold (read 2026-10-17 with tools/gff4.py and ghilana)

- **`<area>.are`**, GFF3 `ARE V3.2` (`designerareas.erf`): `Tag`, `Name` (strref), `ScriptResRefID` (`area_core`), `Layout` (the level: `amn150d`), `MusicPreset`, `ReverbPreset`, `NoTeleport`, `Map`, `North`, and one list per kind of object: `TriggerList`, `CreatureList`, `PlaceableList`, `ItemList`, and (I expect, not seen in this one) waypoints, sounds, stores. Each object: `TemplateResRef`, `Tag`, `XPosition`/`Y`/`Z`, `XOrientation`/`Y`/`Z`/`W` (a quaternion), `Active`, `Importance`, `VarTable` (name, type, value), `TeamId`, and per kind more (`Rank`, `TreasureCategory`, `PickLock`, `WeaponsDrawn`, `InteractRadius`; a trigger's `Geometry`, a polygon of points relative to it). Sample: `coa150ar_moonshine_den.are` (Awakening's, 4 triggers, 3 creatures, 20 placeables).
- **`<level>.arl`**, GFF4 `ARL V4.0` (`packages/core/env/`): `EnvAreaRoomList` (each room's id, file, bounds), `EnvAreaPathfindExport` (below), `EnvAreaCenter`, `EnvAreaSkydomeModel`, `EnvAreaFrameBufEffectList` (`da_bloom`, `da_hsvmatrix` with named parameters), wind (`EnvAreaGlobalwindResRef`, a `.wnd`; `EnvAreaLocalwindList`), terrain chunking (`EnvAreaChunkIsChunk`, rows, columns, 64 m), `EnvAreaStartPointName`, the minimap's corners, sunlight (`EnvAreaSunlightColor`, `...Direction`, `...ColorChar`, `...CanBeOccludedChar`), `EnvAreaTreecontrollerList` (SPT), `EnvTreeColorLevelTint`, `LvlLightSubsetList`, `AtmoData` (ATMO), `CloudData` (CLDS). Sample: `den000d.arl` (2.9 MB, mostly the grid).
- **The walkmesh is a grid, in the `.arl`**, which answers where the `Pathfinding::` environments get their map: `EnvAreaPathfindExport` / `AreaGridArea` (NAVI): `AreaGridNbColumns` 518, `AreaGridNbRows` 387, `AreaGridCellSize` 0.5, `AreaGridClearance` 2.1, `AreaGridBasePos`, `AreaGridNormal`, `AreaGridData` (per cell), `AreaGridHeight`, `AreaGridAbstractSectorSize` 11, `AreaGridAbstractSectors` (2,160 SECT, each with links: the abstraction levels of the hierarchical A*), `AreaGridAbstractMemory`, `AreaGridRoomID`, `AreaGridLink`, `AreaGridLinkID`, `AreaGridLinkRoomID`; and `EnvStatPhys` (SPHY, `EnvStatPhysData`, a blob beginning 208: PhysX's cooked static scene, I'd guess). What a cell's byte means, and how a sector and its links are laid out, is the part to read in the exe (MapSearchEnvironment over this data).

## What Paragon would call

- **`area::Read(gff3 bytes)`**: the area's fields above and its objects, one struct per object with the common fields (kind, template, tag, position, quaternion, active, variables, team) and the kind's own as they come, a trigger's polygon included. No template read: Paragon reads the `.utc`/`.utp` through `gff3` itself, or a later helper.
- **`layout::Read(gff4 bytes)`** (the `.arl`): rooms (id, file, bounds), the sky dome, the frame buffer effects with their parameters, sunlight, wind's name, the chunk grid, the start point's name, the minimap's corners, the tree controllers. Atmosphere and clouds as named floats is plenty for now.
- **`navigation::Grid`** from the `.arl`'s `AreaGridArea`: the grid's size, cell size, base, clearance; per cell whether it's walkable and its height and room; the abstract sectors and their links. Then, as you read it in the exe, the meaning of the cell bytes, which is what Paragon's pathfinding must reproduce (your `World.h` notes on the search's answers and corner cutting are the rules over it).
- Tests over the two samples above and any room-based level (`lak303d.arl`) would cover both kinds of level.

Paragon's milestone 4 (walking around) needs the grid; milestone 2 (an area as lit) needs the layout's sunlight, sky and effects; the objects are milestone 3 and 5. So `layout` first would unblock the most, then `area`, then the grid.
