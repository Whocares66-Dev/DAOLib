# The creatures' AI

What makes a creature act in the vanilla game, and the switches the game has for it. The AI is script, not engine: `rules_core.nss` and `ai_main_h_2.nss`, read from the toolset's source (`dev/TOOLSET.md`; the lines below are in `toolset\extract\scripts\`, under `_Core Includes` and `_Core Scripts`). Its loop, a chain of command-complete events, was first read for the turn-based mode and is in `dev/TURN_BASED.md` ("The AI's loop").

## One creature's AI switched off (read 2026-10-11, seen in play 2026-10-12)

Asked by the multiplayer mod (`C:\project\DAOKadanMP`, its issue 1): a guest's copy of a hostile creature must choose nothing for itself and still carry out the commands it is given. The scripts have a switch for that, and no hook is needed.

- **The switch** is `CREATURE_RULES_FLAG_AI_OFF`, bit `0x00000008` (`core_h.nss`:105) of the creature's local int `CREATURE_RULES_FLAG0` (`var_constants_h.nss`:100). `GetCreatureFlag` and `SetCreatureFlag` are only `GetLocalInt` and `SetLocalInt` on it, so a plugin sets it with `SetLocalInt(creature, "CREATURE_RULES_FLAG0", old | 8)`, keeping the other bits: `0x2` dying, `0x4` damage over time (`dev/TURN_BASED.md`), `0x10` `AI_NO_ABILITIES`, `0x20` no cooldown, `0x40` no resistance, `0x80` and `0x100` a forced combat result. It does not outlive a save and a load: seen in play 2026-10-12 by the multiplayer mod's session, twice (Denerim's market, Caridin's Cross), a party member and two darkspawn with the local at 8 were saved, and after the load all three read 0. Whether the local is not written or is set again as a creature loads has not been read. (This note first said it would be saved, being a local; that was a guess.)
- **Where it is read**: one place, `AI_DetermineCombatRound` (`ai_main_h_2.nss`:392), after the returns for a non-combatant, a game mode that is neither combat nor explore, and a creature with a current command or a queue. With the bit set it returns and queues nothing, not even a wait. A command given to the creature runs, its `EVENT_TYPE_COMMAND_COMPLETE` calls `AI_DetermineCombatRound` (`rules_core.nss`:796), and that returns again.
- **Who sets it**: only the game's debug scripts (`_Debug\ai.nss`:80, `zz_junerelease2.nss`:23). No shipped content does.
- **Seen in play** by the multiplayer mod's session, 2026-10-12, GOG's game, two instances on one save (Caridin's Cross after the ambush, 79 creatures), through Eluvian. Three darkspawn (their local 0 and `AI_CUSTOM_AI_ACTIVE` 0) were put 6 to 8 m from the party in each game, flagged in one. Without the flag all three fought at once: 28 commands started in 12 s, attacks and abilities. With it each had combat state 1, the game was in combat mode, and none started a command in 12 s. A flagged genlock given `dao::orders::Attack` struck once, the command ended, and it started nothing after, while its target killed it; the same with the scripts' `CommandAttack` queued by `AddCommand`. A creature on custom AI has not been tried.

What does not read the bit, from the source:

- **Custom AI.** With the local `AI_CUSTOM_AI_ACTIVE` non-zero, `rules_core` sends the creature's own script `EVENT_TYPE_HANDLE_CUSTOM_AI` (1016) in place of `AI_DetermineCombatRound` (`rules_core.nss`:671, 787, 953). Scripted fights use it: Howe's men, the Tower of Ishal's emissary, Uldred, the climax's armies. Zero is `CAI_DISABLED`.
- **`rules_core`'s own commands.** A creature first perceiving a hostile is put in combat state and all its commands are cleared (`rules_core.nss`:940-941), a command given just before among them; it then queues a wait if the enemy is over 25 m away and its weapon is not a melee one (977). The same event clears and moves each non-follower ally not yet in combat toward the enemy (1054-1058), from the perceiver's event and not the ally's. A creature damaged out of combat by someone it does not perceive is cleared and moved to the damager (333-339). None of these showed as a command started on the three flagged creatures in the play above.
- **Ambient behaviour** out of combat (`Ambient_CommandComplete`, `rules_core.nss`:802; `Ambient_SpawnStart`, 1325). Its own switch is the local `AMBIENT_SYSTEM_STATE` at 0 (`wrappers_h.nss`:371).
- **A creature's own script** that handles an event before passing it to `rules_core`.

Two things that are not this switch. `AI_LIGHT_ACTIVE` selects `AI_DetermineCombatRound_Light` (`ai_main_h_2.nss`:403, 767), a cheaper AI that still picks a target and attacks; the off bit is tested before it. `SetAILevel` (command 222) writes `CCObject` +0x2B4 and `GetAILevel` reads +0x2B0, and what the engine does with the level has not been read; the scripts set it only around resurrection (`rules_core.nss`:1197, `effect_resurrection_h.nss`:40) and in one plot script.
